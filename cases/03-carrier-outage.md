# Carrier outage and escalation

**Type:** Guided simulation. **Jira outcome:** Escalated, then Resolved with Fixed resolution.

## Simulated findings

Branch 3 lost connectivity to HQ. The router remained reachable through separate management access, but the WAN interface was down/down. Onsite power and cabling checks passed. The carrier confirmed an outage without a restoration estimate.

## Escalation record

Training carrier case LAB-3001 was recorded with a next update due in 30 minutes. The ticket remained open while awaiting restoration. No real carrier case was opened.

## Simulated recovery

After the carrier reported restoration, the exercise supplied an up/up WAN interface, successful branch-to-HQ connectivity, user confirmation of HQ application access, cleared alerts, and a stable connection during verification.

## Ticketing lesson

Escalate to the party able to correct the evidenced fault. Continue tracking the incident and verify restoration rather than resolving solely on the carrier's statement.
